Why we're signing

  • Overheating risk — balconies are the only safe airflow many flats have; losing them puts children and vulnerable residents at real risk in hot weather.
  • No outdoor space — the playground gates are locked, so upstairs balconies are the only private outdoor space some families have.
  • Belongings locked away — residents' own items and storage kept on balconies have been made inaccessible without consent.
  • Paying more, getting less — balconies were sold and let as a feature; removing them cuts living space and property value without fair resolution.

What we're asking for: pause all balcony removal works now, and hold a proper consultation with residents on repair or upgrade options — not full removal. For many of us, this is the final straw after months of unresolved conditions.
