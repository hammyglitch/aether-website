What Are
SOPs?

A Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is a detailed set of instructions that guides employees through a specific task to achieve consistent results. SOPs are essential for maintaining quality, compliance, and operational efficiency — especially in regulated industries like healthcare, finance, and manufacturing. SOPs reduce process variability by up to 65% (WorkflowAutomation.net 2025) and improve compliance audit pass rates by 40% (Scribe.com 2025).

The Aether
SOP Framework

Every SOP we build follows a proven structure designed for clarity, compliance, and automation readiness.

1. Purpose

Clearly states the SOP's objective and scope. Why does this procedure exist? What problem does it solve?

2. Scope

Defines who this SOP applies to, what systems it covers, and any exceptions or limitations.

3. Roles

Identifies who is responsible for each step. Clear accountability. No ambiguity about who does what.

4. Procedure

Sequential instructions with sub-steps, diagrams, and checklists. Written in clear, active language.

5. Tools & References

Links to related documents, software, and resources. Everything in one place.

6. Review History

Tracks updates and authorizations. Version control ensures everyone follows the latest procedure.
