Why we're petitioning the Council

  • Recorded indoor temperatures of up to 39°C for 10+ hours once balcony airflow is lost — a real health risk to children and vulnerable residents.
  • The downstairs playground gates remain locked with no access granted to residents, leaving balconies as the only private outdoor space for many families.
  • Residents' own belongings and storage kept on balconies have been locked away and made inaccessible during these works, without residents' consent.
  • Loss of daylight, fresh air and space for plants directly affects residents' wellbeing.
  • Balconies were let and sold as a feature of these flats — removing them without fair resolution affects rent value and property value alike.
  • 44 residents of Pegasus Close are already paying in excess of £100 per month between them for just 9 parking spaces — this balcony removal compounds an already unfair set of conditions on this close.

We're asking the Council to pause all balcony removal works and hold a formal consultation with residents to explore repair or upgrade options instead of full removal. Every signature below is added to the register submitted to the Council Planning & Housing Department. For many residents on this close, the balcony removal is the final straw after months of unresolved conditions.
