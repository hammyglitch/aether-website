What is the ROI of AI automation?
The evidence is consistent and strong. UK businesses using AI automation report 80% reduction in admin tasks (Gartner 2025) and 10× throughput increase (McKinsey 2025). Average ROI across Deloitte's 2025 benchmarks: 300% within 12 months. The cost of not automating — in wasted staff hours, errors, and missed scale — consistently outweighs the investment in building it.
Will AI replace my employees?
AI replaces tasks, not people. It handles routine, repetitive, low-judgment work so your team can focus on strategy, relationships, and the decisions that actually require a human. 67% of UK businesses plan to deploy AI agents by 2026 (PwC 2025) — and those that do consistently report higher employee satisfaction, because staff stop doing the work they hate. The businesses most at risk are those that let competitors deploy AI while they wait.
What tools and platforms do you integrate with?
We integrate with virtually any platform that has an API or webhook: ClickUp, Google Workspace, Fireflies, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Xero, Shopify, Notion, Airtable, Make, Zapier, and more. API-first architecture means if a platform has an endpoint, we can connect it. The UK cloud market grew 28% in 2025 (Gartner) — most modern business tools are already integration-ready.
How do you ensure AI accuracy and reliability?
Every AI system we build is tested against real business scenarios before deployment, with a human approval layer for all critical decisions. We use SOP-based instruction sets so AI agents follow your exact documented processes — not generic behaviour. Audit trails are built in by default. There are no black boxes: every decision is traceable, every output reviewable. We also run parallel operation periods where the automated system runs alongside the manual process until confidence is established.
